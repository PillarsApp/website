Frequently Asked Questions

What Are The Namaz Timings In London Today?

London namaz timings today are Fajr 06:00, Sunrise 07:36, Dhuhr 12:50, Asr 15:20 (Hanafi 16:02), Maghrib 17:56, Isha 19:17. The list at the top of this page shows them with the current prayer, and the arrows step to other days.

What Time Is Fajr In London Today?

Fajr in London today begins at 06:00 and lasts until sunrise at 07:36. When fasting, suhoor ends as Fajr begins, and the fast is opened at Maghrib, 17:56.

Why Do London Prayer Times Differ Between Apps And Mosques?

Apps and mosques use different calculation methods. This page uses the Moonsighting Committee method with Fajr and Isha both at 18°, while many London mosques follow the London Unified Prayer Timetable. The difference is usually a few minutes for Dhuhr, Asr and Maghrib and larger for Fajr and Isha.

Which Timetable Do London Mosques Follow?

Many London mosques, including East London Mosque and London Central Mosque, publish the London Unified Prayer Timetable. It is based on local observation rather than a fixed sun angle, so its Fajr and Isha can differ from the times on this page by several minutes, most of all in summer. Both are valid ways of working out the times. For jamaat (congregation) times, follow your local mosque.

What Is The Asr Time In London For Hanafis?

London has two Asr times. Today the Hanafi Asr in London is at 16:02, 42 minutes after the Shafi'i Asr at 15:20. Hanafi Asr starts when an object's shadow is twice its length; the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali time starts at once its length.

What Time Is Jumu'ah In London?

Jumu'ah is prayed in place of Dhuhr. On Friday 23 October 2026 Dhuhr in London begins at 12:50. Each mosque sets its own khutbah and jamaat times, so check with the mosque you attend.

Why Is Isha So Late In London In Summer?

London is far enough north that the sky barely gets fully dark around midsummer. On Monday 21 June 2027, Fajr is at 02:43, Maghrib at 21:25 and Isha at 22:42. The Moonsighting Committee method applies seasonal limits so Isha and Fajr stay at a practical time.

What Is The Qibla Direction In London?

The Qibla from London is 119° from true north, roughly east-southeast. That is the shortest path across the globe to the Kaaba in Makkah, which is why it points east-southeast rather than straight towards Saudi Arabia on a flat map.

Are These Times In GMT Or BST?

All times are United Kingdom local time, currently BST. The clocks change on Sunday 25 October 2026; from that day the times are in GMT, and the timetable already accounts for it.
