Our Mission

“Indeed, Allah will not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves.”

Qur’an, Ar-Ra’d 13:11

﷽

We started Pillars as students, after learning that the data of Muslims using prayer apps was being sold on. A prayer app knows where you are, five times a day. We believe looking after that is an amaanah, a trust.

So we built the app we wanted for our own families: no ads, your location kept on your phone, and nothing standing between you and the prayer.

Abdul-Rahman & Omar

Founders of Pillars
